How it works

Your team approves. The agent does the work.

Most teams lose hours to copying, checking and chasing. Your email, documents and apps feed one workflow, and the agent spots the work, drafts the action and sends it to the right person.

Once approved, it carries the work across your connected systems. Every action is logged, so you can see what it did, when and why.

You stay in command. We handle the coordination.

Frequently asked questions

Where do we start?

Book the free AI Fit Call. If there is a workflow worth automating, the AI Audit maps it and gives you a fixed quote.

What does it cost?

The AI Audit is $1,500 and is credited to your Pilot. Pilots start at $6,000 and production at $15,000 per workflow. Claude and cloud usage are billed at cost.

Will the AI act without our approval?

Not until you decide it should. Every agent starts in shadow mode, then supervised, and works on its own only after it passes the checks we agree with you.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, prompts, infrastructure and runbooks are handed over at the end of every project.

Do we need engineers on our side?

No. We build and run it, and train your team to extend it.

Will it replace our systems?

No. It works on top of the tools you already use.

Which AI do you use?

Claude, by Anthropic, through your Claude workspace or Amazon Bedrock in your own AWS account.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. We work in English and Spanish with teams across the US and Latin America.
